THE STORY

One newcomer. One irreversible act. And a town that would rather keep its secrets.

0 M
38 M

A NEW LIGHT.

A Girl moves to an isolated mining town. Through her eyes, the place is strange, magnetic and full of possibility. She meets the Son, who is still learning what kind of man he wants to become.

TWO MEN. TWO KINDS OF LOYALTY.

The Son looks up to his larger-than-life Father and the charming, influential Ghost. Each offers a different idea of what loyalty means.

THE TOWN HAS ITS OWN RULES.

Claims, favours and debts bind the town together. The Ghost settles disputes. The Father chases one final big find. Everybody knows more than they say.

THE SURFACE BREAKS.

When the Girl is harmed by an older man the town prefers not to see, its apparent innocence fractures. The Son cannot look away.

ONE CHOICE CANNOT BE UNDONE.

The Son goes out to confront the man. The man does not return. The Father hides what happened. The Ghost takes the boy's side.

THE TRUTH DIVIDES THEM.

Father and Son are driven apart. The Mother sees the damage spreading through the family. What happened to the Girl has revealed what was buried in everyone else.

The full treatment contains mature themes, including violence and sexual assault. The incident is not depicted on screen.
